Open chat to copy and exchange your public encryption key with the person you want to contact. Share public keys through a trusted channel; never share private keys. Add the other person's public key under **Manage contacts** so you can start future conversations without pasting it again.

[Open chat](command:devChat.openChat)

After both people have connected to the same relay and saved each other's public keys, select the contact in chat and send a short test message.
